## 2. Five Plain-English Client Protections & Service Terms

### A. 14-Day Free Proof Pilot & 3x Minimum ROI Guarantee
1. **Zero Upfront Cost (`$0.00` for Days 1–14):** Your first 14 calendar days are a **100% Free Proof Pilot**. You pay nothing on Day 1.
2. **The 3x Minimum ROI Guarantee:** Before any monthly fee is billed on Day 15 (and in every monthly billing cycle thereafter), MarginVault AI delivers your **Found-Money ROI Scorecard** itemizing verified wholesale overcharge credits, register margin recovery, delivery chargeback wins, and prep/waste reductions. **If your verified monthly savings and recovered profit do not equal at least three times (`3x`) your monthly service fee, you pay `$0.00` for that month.**

### B. Founding Store Rate Lock Guarantee
* As one of MarginVault AI's Founding Bay Area Stores, your selected monthly rate (**`$199/mo`**, **`$399/mo`**, or **`$699/mo`**) is **permanently locked in** for as long as your subscription remains active—even when standard rates increase to `$499–$1,950/mo` for future cohorts.

### C. Read-Only Advisory Scope & 100% Owner Final Approval
* **You Stay in 100% Control of Your Registers, Menus & Bank Accounts:** MarginVault AI operates strictly as a **read-only back-office data analyst and profit advisor**. MarginVault AI never initiates bank transfers, never alters live POS register prices without your authorization, and never signs supplier contracts on your behalf.
* **Final Owner Verification:** All SMS/email register price updates, 8:00 PM bake/prep sheets, distributor credit requests, delivery menu markups, and tax/CRV exemption summaries are provided as decision-support recommendations. **The Store Owner retains 100% final approval and sole responsibility** for verifying and applying POS register prices, menu prices, food safety/allergen compliance, ABC/alcoholic beverage pricing rules, and official CPA/tax filings.
* **Limitation of Liability:** Both parties agree that MarginVault AI LLC's maximum aggregate liability for any claim arising under this Agreement is strictly capped at **one (1) month's service fee** paid by Client in the month preceding the claim.

### D. 5-Layer Data Privacy, Confidentiality & 100% Client Data Ownership
* **100% Client Ownership:** You retain **100% ownership** of all supplier invoices, recipes, cost books, and POS sales exports shared with MarginVault AI. Your vendor pricing is strictly confidential and will never be shared in an identifiable way with any competitor.
* **Local PII & Banking Firewall:** Before any invoice photo or CSV is analyzed, MarginVault AI automatically masks bank account numbers, routing numbers (ACH), federal tax IDs (FEIN/SSN), and personal home addresses locally via our `LocalPIIBankingFirewall` (`pii_banking_scrubber.py`). ### E. Month-to-Month Flexibility (No Long-Term Lock-In)

* **Easy Cancellation:** You may pause or cancel your subscription at any time—with zero cancellation penalties—by sending a simple text message or email to `support@marginvaultai.com` at least **15 days before your next billing date**. Upon cancellation, all copies of your store's invoices and POS exports are permanently deleted or returned to you within 48 hours.
